A story of searching for the perfect taste

CARTEL began in 2004 — a story of constant improvement and of the people who create a new standard of leisure in Ukraine.

From the first restaurant to a hospitality group

In 2004 we launched our first gastronomy projects in Bukovel.

Over the years CARTEL has opened more than 18 restaurants — Ukrainian, Italian, Asian and American — and the Eat’n’Go! quick-service chain. Current gastronomy trends, excellent service and a special atmosphere are the three pillars of the CARTEL restaurant family.

Today CARTEL goes far beyond food service: the group includes a SPA complex, a traditional wood-fired bathhouse, hotels and real estate.

Milestones

  1. 2004

    First gastronomy projects in Bukovel

    The CARTEL restaurant family starts at the resort.

  2. 2014

    A page of history

    Until 2014 CARTEL also ran restaurants in Foros. Because of the occupation of Crimea, the company lost the ability to operate Avtohraf, Olivia and Albatros.

  3. 2015

    VODA club opens

    A leisure and SPA complex on Lake Molodosti.

  4. Today

    A hospitality group

    Restaurants, hotels, SPA and investment projects in Bukovel, Yaremche and Odesa.

Our philosophy of hospitality

Taste. Rest. Enjoy.

Our core idea is to let Ukrainians enjoy affordable holidays at home with a quality of service that even Europeans would envy. We believe in healthy, high-quality leisure.
