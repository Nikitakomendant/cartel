LE GRAND RESTAURANT

European cuisine and Carpathian classics in the lobby of Mountain Residence.

Book a tableMenu

A special place in the lobby of Mountain Residence Hotel Wellness & Spa, with breathtaking views and atmosphere.

Our chefs found their inspiration in the French Alps, among the best ski resorts, and carefully brought the finest European dishes to the menu — together with gems of Carpathian gastronomy gathered among the peaks of the Chornohora ridge, presented on a separate page of the menu.

A private wine room with an outstanding wine list completes a dinner with a view of the Gorgany mountains.
