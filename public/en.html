About CARTEL

In 2004 we launched our first gastronomy projects in Bukovel. Today CARTEL is a hospitality group that brings together restaurants of Ukrainian, Italian, Asian and American cuisine, hotels, the VODA club SPA complex, the wood-fired bathhouse “Banka na Drovakh” and investment projects.

We keep growing to create a new standard of leisure in Ukraine.
